\noindent This note reports the measurements and pairs each with the theory part it
confirms, refines, or bounds. The theory document is the proven framework; here we say
what the experiments show, and we are explicit about what is proven, what is measured,
and what is only suggestive. Tiers: an \emph{MLP toy} (fast, with exact provenance tags)
and the \emph{LLM tier} (Qwen2.5-7B, LoRA SFT-KL, real Pokec graph and profiles).

\section{The loop}
Each round: a nonlinear bounded-confidence population (Hegselmann--Krause, or
Deffuant with algorithmic bias) updates; a model is retrained on the current
(platform-mediated) opinions, anchored to a fixed prior with strength $\beta$ (the
SFT-KL coefficient); the model feeds a \emph{per-node} prediction $\mu_i$ back, absorbed
only by agents within the confidence radius $\varepsilon$. Contamination $s$ is the
fraction of opinion that is recycled model output.

\section{Contamination follows the identity (Parts A, C)}
\emph{MLP, exact.} The gated identity
$\Delta s_t = w\,\mathrm{contact}_t\,\langle1-f\rangle_{G_t}$ holds to machine precision;
the measured law $ds/dt\propto\mathrm{contact}\,(1-s)$ is its uncorrelated case; forced
detachment stalls $s$ at the captured fraction ($s\approx0.69$), as the covariance
correction predicts. The degeneracy (retraining does not move $s$) is verified
symbolically and numerically. These are the proven core (Part A) made visible.

\section{The $\bm\beta$ switch: displacement vs collapse (Part B)}
\emph{LLM, two seeds --- the headline.} The KL anchor selects the failure mode:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\beta=0$: population diversity \emph{contracts} (std $0.094\to0.067$ at
$\varepsilon=0.4$) with small mean displacement ($+0.05$) --- \textbf{collapse};
\item $\beta=3$: diversity is \emph{maintained/widened} (std $0.114\to0.153$) with large
displacement ($+0.17$ toward the prior) --- \textbf{displacement without collapse}.
\end{itemize}
This is the gated, nonlinear realization of the \emph{mechanism} in Part B's variance
decomposition $\Var(x_\infty)=(1-s)^2\Var(x_0)+s^2\Var(\bm\mu)+2s(1-s)\,$Cov (which is
itself a linear-model identity): collapse is the concentrated-prediction case
($\Var(\bm\mu)\to0$, $\beta=0$), displacement the spread-prediction case
($\Var(\bm\mu)>0$, $\beta=3$). The qualitative driver --- whether the model's predictions
are uniform or spread --- transfers from the linear formula to the gated experiment;
the closed form itself does not (the gated dynamics also carry the floor of Part~E). The
per-node structure is load-bearing either way: a scalar-broadcast model could only ever
collapse.

\section{Gate-monotone, loop-caused collapse (Parts C, D)}
\emph{LLM, two seeds.} Model-side collapse is measured by $\ell_{c0}$, the current
model's loss on the frozen round-0 data (forgetting of the original distribution). Peak
$\ell_{c0}$ rises monotonically with the gate:
\[
\text{no-feedback }1.25 \;<\; \varepsilon{=}.1\ 1.85 \;<\; .2\ 2.59 \;<\; .3\ 3.36 \;<\; .4\ 3.98 .
\]
The flat no-feedback control ($\ell_{c0}$ barely moves) proves the loop causes the
forgetting; the trust radius $\varepsilon$ sets the depth. This is the model-side
shadow of Part~D's capture region: wider $\varepsilon$ captures more of the population,
so the model trains on more-collapsed data. Reproduced across both seeds; the
$\varepsilon{=}.3$ vs $.4$ ordering, unsettled at 30 rounds, resolves
($\varepsilon{=}.4$ deeper) at 60.

\section{The diversity floor (Part E)}
Captured populations do not reach zero spread: the residual std is $0.07$--$0.10$,
matching the floor $A^2\sigma_m^2$ with $\sigma_m$ the model's per-node prediction
spread. Diversity reaches zero only when predictions concentrate ($\sigma_m\to0$),
tying back to the $\beta$ switch.

\section{The anchor does double duty}
$\beta$ does not only prevent collapse --- it also \emph{stabilizes training}. At
$\beta=0$, continual SFT destabilizes at long horizons (around round 50, the loss on the
current batch jumps as the model stops fitting its own data); every $\beta>0$ run stays
stable. The clean collapse metric is therefore the \emph{peak} $\ell_{c0}$, not the
final value.

\section{Collapse depth: the fraction-saturated corner (P5, honest)}
The natural prediction $\ell_{c0}\sim s^2$ \textbf{fails}, and instructively. Contamination
saturates ($s\to1$ in every loop cell), so the synthetic \emph{fraction} is pinned and
cannot explain the $\varepsilon$-ladder. The depth instead tracks the training-data
\emph{quality}: $\ell_{c0}$ rises as the batch variance falls
($0.016\to0.005$ as $\ell_{c0}\,1.85\to3.98$). The decisive pair: no-feedback and
$\varepsilon{=}.2$ share the same batch variance but differ in $\ell_{c0}$ ($1.25$ vs
$2.59$) --- because one trains on real data ($s=0$) and the other on recycled data
($s\approx1$). So in the two-factor law $\ell_{c0}=\text{base}+s^2 c^2$, the fraction
$s^2$ is a \emph{switch} (real vs recycled) and the quality gap $c^2$ carries all the
$\varepsilon$-dependence. Our loop retrains on the fully-mediated population every round,
so it sits in the fraction-saturated, quality-varying corner --- distinct from the
fraction-dialing regime of the model-collapse literature. The bridge holds structurally;
the precise $c^2$-vs-quality form awaits more seeds (and is muddied by the $\beta=0$
instability).

\section{Real-data validation}
\emph{Pokec} (unimodal innate, sparse graph): the $\varepsilon$ axis survives ---
threshold, gated capture, stranded minority --- while the algorithmic-bias ($\gamma$)
axis is inert (the graph is too sparse and the population too unimodal to fragment).
\emph{PolitiSky24} (real polarized 2024-election stances on a dense interaction graph):
$\gamma$ matters; a centrist-prior platform merges both camps at its prior; a
partisan-prior platform flips the majority camp at wide $\varepsilon$ and strands roughly
half of it at narrow $\varepsilon$. Caveats: PolitiSky labels are LLM-annotated and the
camps are 13:1 imbalanced.

\section{Limitations}
Most cells are one or two seeds. The $\beta=0$ long-horizon instability corrupts the
collapse tail (we report the peak). The inherited platform susceptibility is high, so
magnitudes are conditional on strong trust. The $\gamma$-healing result needs a denser
real graph to test fairly. LLM contamination $s$ is reconstructed from the contact
trajectory via the Part-A identity, not directly tagged; a provenance tag in the pipeline
would let us measure it.

\section{Status}
Proven and verified (Parts A--E): contamination is value-blind with closed-form $s$ and a
degeneracy; per-node displacement and the three-term variance; the gated $s$-identity;
the capture/detachment boundary $\varepsilon/\kappa$; the diversity floor $A^2\sigma_m^2$.
Measured and reproduced (two seeds): the $\beta$ switch, gate-monotone loop-caused
collapse, the floor magnitude, the anchor's stabilizing role. Refined, not closed: the
collapse-depth law (P5, fraction-saturated corner). Conjectural: the
performative-stable-point framing (P6), with the caveat that the closed-form KL result is
RL-KL while our experiments are SFT-KL.
